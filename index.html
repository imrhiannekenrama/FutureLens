About FutureLens

Stories for the space between now and next.

FutureLens is an entertainment experience that creates personalized fictional future readings based on your answers.

We are clear about the line: FutureLens does not have supernatural powers, cannot predict real events, and does not need sensitive personal information to create a reading.

Privacy Policy

FutureLens is designed to work in your browser. Your reading answers are used to create your fictional result and may be kept only for the current browser session so a refresh does not interrupt your experience. Do not enter sensitive personal information. We do not ask for passwords, banking information, government IDs, or exact addresses.

Terms of Use

FutureLens is for entertainment and inspiration only. Readings are fictional creative outputs and are not professional, financial, medical, legal, relationship, or life advice. Use your own judgment for every real-world decision.

For entertainment purposes only. FutureLens creates fictional personalized readings and does not actually predict the future.
